FAQ

Questions families ask

Is this an exam? Can a student fail?+

No. It's an aptitude assessment with no pass or fail. It shows strengths and areas to grow, and never tells a student they can't do something.

Who is it for?+

Students in Class 9 to 12: Class 10 students choosing a stream, and Class 12 students choosing a degree, entrance exams and colleges.

What is the assessment based on?+

Ability questions in the style of standard aptitude batteries, and a short version of the Holland (RIASEC) interest model used by career counsellors worldwide. Research links ability to job performance and interest fit to job satisfaction, so Dishaa uses both.

When can students talk to mentors, alumni and counsellors?+

We're onboarding alumni, mentors and career counsellors now, and our prep materials are in preparation. They open after our pilot with 5 schools in Bengaluru. Students can ask to be told when each one opens.

How do students and parents get access?+

Dishaa is a paid service. Once a school or family subscribes, we create the accounts and email each person their login details.

Where do college fees, cutoffs, salaries and exam dates come from?+

From published sources, each shown with its source and the date it was checked. They change every year, so we always link to the official site.

Is student data private?+

Yes. Results are visible only to the student, their linked parent and their school counsellor. Scholarship details are optional and used only to find scholarships.
