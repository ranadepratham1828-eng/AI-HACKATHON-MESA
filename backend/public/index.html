One of the biggest decisions a student makes, made almost blind

Aptitude assessment

Measures what students can do, not just what they say

Logical, numerical, verbal and spatial reasoning, memory, critical thinking, creativity and people skills. Students also rate themselves, and the report shows where their self-image and their results differ.

Career matches

Careers matched to abilities and interests

Each match explains why it fits, then lays out the route: stream after Class 10, degree and entrance exams, colleges with fees, cutoffs and NIRF ranks, and first jobs after the degree.

AI career coach and plan

A counsellor available to every student

The coach talks the student through their options, including family expectations, then builds a personalised plan: timeline, exam preparation, dream, target and safe colleges within budget, scholarships to apply for, and the next 30 days. Studying abroad is supported too.

For parents

Parents see their child's strengths next to their own hopes, where they match and where they differ, with conversation starters in English, Kannada or Hindi.

FAQ

Questions families ask

Is this an exam? Can a student fail?

No. It's an aptitude assessment with no pass or fail. It shows strengths and areas to grow, and never tells a student they can't do something.

Who is it for?

Students in Class 9-12: Class 10 students choosing a stream, and Class 12 students choosing a degree, entrance exams and colleges.

How do students and parents get access?

Dishaa is a paid service. Once a school or family subscribes, we create the accounts and email each person their login details.

Where does the college and scholarship information come from?

From official and published sources, each shown with its source and the date it was checked. Fees and cutoffs change every year, so we always link to the official site.

Is student data private?

Yes. Students' results are visible only to the student, their linked parent and their school counsellor. Scholarship details are optional and used only to find scholarships.
