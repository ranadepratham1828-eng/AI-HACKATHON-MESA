How it works

From "I'm not sure" to a plan in four steps

  1. About you

    Class, subjects, marks, interests, careers already on their mind, priorities and budget.

  2. Assessment

    27 interactive questions measure 9 abilities, with a Holland (RIASEC) interest check.

  3. Report and matches

    Strengths with evidence, and 36 careers ranked by fit, each with salary, pros and cons.

  4. Coach and plan

    A conversation to settle the goal, then a plan from today to the first job.

Assessment

Measures what a student can do, and compares it with what they think

Logical, numerical, verbal, spatial and mechanical reasoning, memory, critical thinking, creativity and people skills. Students rate themselves first, and the report shows where their self-image and their results differ.

Career matches

36 careers, with the trade-offs stated plainly

Each match shows why it fits, the stream, degrees and entrance exams, early salary in India, job demand, and honest pros and cons. Careers the student already has in mind are always shown, with their real fit.

Coach and plan

A counsellor available to every student

The AI coach talks through the options, including what the family hopes for, then builds a plan: timeline, exam preparation, dream, target and safe colleges within budget, scholarships and the next 30 days. Studying abroad is included.

Parents

The whole family looking at the same facts

Parents see their child's strengths next to their own hopes, where they match and where they differ, with conversation starters in English, Kannada or Hindi.

FAQ

Questions families ask

Is this an exam? Can a student fail?

No. It's an aptitude assessment with no pass or fail. It shows strengths and areas to grow, and never tells a student they can't do something.

Who is it for?

Students in Class 9 to 12: Class 10 students choosing a stream, and Class 12 students choosing a degree, entrance exams and colleges.

What is the assessment based on?

Ability questions in the style of standard aptitude batteries, and a short version of the Holland (RIASEC) interest model used by career counsellors worldwide. Research links ability to job performance and interest fit to job satisfaction, so Dishaa uses both.

How do students and parents get access?

Dishaa is a paid service. Once a school or family subscribes, we create the accounts and email each person their login details.

Where do college fees, cutoffs and salaries come from?

From published sources, each shown with its source and the date it was checked. Fees and cutoffs change every year, so we always link to the official site.

Is student data private?

Yes. Results are visible only to the student, their linked parent and their school counsellor. Scholarship details are optional and used only to find scholarships.
